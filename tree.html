How the tree grows.

The giving tree is more than a giving menu — it is a living mural. A large, hand-painted version of this tree will be installed inside the market barn at 201 Bedford Road. Every gift, at every level, will be inscribed somewhere on the tree:

  • ·
    Friends and Patrons are inscribed on the roots and soil, the foundation that holds everything up.
  • ·
    Seedlings and Sprouts are inscribed on the trunk, supporting the tree's full weight.
  • ·
    Saplings, Harvests, and Founders Circles are inscribed on individual branches, each one a story of one family's commitment.
  • ·
    Harvest Patrons and Legacy Partners are inscribed in the canopy, where the tree comes into full bloom.
  • ·
    The Naming Sponsor is named in the golden crown — the highest, most visible recognition the campaign can offer.

Children at the school will help paint and tend their portion of the tree each year. By the time the first cohort graduates, the tree will hold every name that helped make the school possible.
