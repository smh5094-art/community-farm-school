Designate your gift.

When you give, you can tell us where your gift should go. Our $1M campaign goal funds the property and the build; everything above $1M goes to students and teachers, and we'll honor any designation you put in writing on the memo line of a check, in the notes field of an online gift, or in an email.

  • ·
    Greatest need — let us direct your gift wherever the campaign needs it most (default if no designation is given).
  • ·
    Property & build — earmarked toward the $750k land purchase or the $250k build budget.
  • ·
    Student scholarship fund — need-based tuition support for families who could not otherwise attend. Activated once we cross $1M.
  • ·
    Teacher compensation & benefits — better pay, health coverage, paid planning time, and continuing-education stipends for our educators.
  • ·
    Scholarship endowment — a permanent endowed fund whose annual draw provides scholarships in perpetuity. Lead-naming available for a founding gift of $250k+ — the fund is named for your family or foundation.

Have questions about how designated funds are managed, or want to set up a charitable remainder trust or family-foundation grant that benefits the school in perpetuity? Jenna would be glad to talk.

Check or online.

The simplest way. Mail a check made payable to Community Farm School to 201 Bedford Road, Carlisle, MA 01741, or give online once our secure donation page is published. Both are processed and acknowledged within a week, with a tax receipt for your records.

Stock and appreciated assets.

Donating long-held appreciated stock, mutual funds, or ETFs is often the most tax-efficient way to give. You avoid capital gains tax on the appreciation, deduct the full fair-market value, and we receive 100% of the gift. We can provide DTC transfer instructions on request — just email Jenna.

IRA Qualified Charitable Distribution.

If you’re 70½ or older, you can give directly from your IRA via a Qualified Charitable Distribution — up to $108,000 per individual in 2026. The gift counts toward your required minimum distribution and is excluded from your taxable income, which can lower your tax bracket and reduce Medicare premium surcharges.

Corporate matching.

Many employers match charitable gifts dollar-for-dollar — some at 2× or 3×. If you work in tech, finance, healthcare, or a Fortune 1000 company, there’s a strong chance your gift can be doubled. Submit your gift through your HR matching portal using our 501(c)(3) name and EIN (available on request).

Donor-advised funds.

If you have a DAF at Fidelity Charitable, Schwab Charitable, Vanguard Charitable, or a community foundation, you can grant directly to Community Farm School. We’re a registered 501(c)(3) and appear in all major DAF databases.

Multi-year pledges.

Major gifts can be pledged across two to five years to align with your household’s tax planning. We’ll work with you to structure a payment schedule that makes sense — named-piece sponsorships count as fully claimed once the pledge is signed.

Community Farm School is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization. All gifts are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law. EIN available upon request. Please consult your tax advisor about the deductibility of any specific contribution.
