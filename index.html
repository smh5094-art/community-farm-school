Community Farm School is a Reggio Emilia–inspired outdoor school where children spend their days among gardens, animals, and a working farm landscape. After years operating at Clark Farm, we’re acquiring our own 18-acre home at 201 Bedford Road in Carlisle.

We need to raise $750,000 for the property and $250,000 for what makes the school come alive: three yurt classrooms, a 26-foot welcome bridge, a greenhouse, a chicken coop, and a natural playground that grows from the soil up.

Our stretch goal is $1.25M–$1.5M. Every dollar raised above $1M goes directly to student scholarships and better pay and benefits for our teachers — the people who make Community Farm School what it is.

Ways to give.

Community Farm School welcomes gifts in many forms. All gifts are tax-deductible under our 501(c)(3) status.

  • ·
    Check or online — mail a check made out to Community Farm School, or give securely online once our donation page is published.
  • ·
    Stock & appreciated assets — avoid capital gains and deduct the full market value. We can provide DTC instructions on request.
  • ·
    IRA Qualified Charitable Distribution — if you’re 70½ or older, you can give directly from your IRA — up to $108,000 in 2026.
  • ·
    Corporate matching — many employers match dollar-for-dollar, sometimes 2× or 3×. Check with your HR team.
  • ·
    Donor-advised funds — grant directly from your DAF using our EIN.
  • ·
    Multi-year pledges — spread a major gift across two to five years so it works for your household.
