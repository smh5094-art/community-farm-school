# Community Farm School — Capital Campaign Site

A private campaign preview site for the Community Farm School capital campaign in Carlisle, MA. The school is raising $1M to acquire 201 Bedford Road and build out the property — with a stretch goal of $1.25–$1.5M to fund student scholarships and teacher benefits.

**Live site:** https://smh5094-art.github.io/community-farm-school/

The site is unlisted (`noindex, nofollow`) during the quiet phase of the campaign. Search engines will not index it; the link is shareable directly with donors.
