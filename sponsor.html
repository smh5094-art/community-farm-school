Corporate Founders · Host your event at the market

$25,000 – $250,000+

For a decade, the post-and-beam Clark Farm Market barn at 201 Bedford Road has welcomed the community for blueberry festivals, holiday markets, and farm gatherings. As the property becomes the permanent home of Community Farm School, we are inviting a small group of corporate partners to help us preserve it — and as a thank you, your company is welcome to host one private event per year at the market, free of charge, for the duration of your tier. The benefit includes use of the market barn, the grounds, and our 30′ event tent (subject to the school calendar).

  • Naming Sponsor — The Market at Community Farm School  $250,000+

    1 available · perpetual naming on the barn · one private event per year for the life of the market

  • Legacy Partner  $100,000

    3 available · permanent plaque inside the market · one private event per year for 10 years

  • Harvest Patron  $50,000

    5 available · recognition plaque · one private event per year for 5 years

  • Founders Circle  $25,000

    10 available · engraved listing on the donor wall · one private event per year for 3 years

Hosted events typically accommodate 75–150 guests under the tent plus the barn. Event dates are coordinated with the school calendar and confirmed at least 60 days in advance. Catering, rentals beyond the included tent, and clean-up are arranged by the sponsoring company. Memorial and family-foundation gifts are welcomed at every tier.

Corporate gifts that push the campaign past $1M flow directly into our student scholarship fund and teacher benefits — a second legacy beyond your name on the barn.
